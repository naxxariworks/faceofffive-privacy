Faceoff Five privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Faceoff Five does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

Who we are

Faceoff Five is a hockey training app for Android, developed by Naxxari Works (József Köller) (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

What the app saves on your phone

To work, the app keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your device:

  • your training settings and appearance choices (colors, background);
  • your training history: date and time, settings, number of targets and how often each spot was called;
  • a paused training session, so you can continue it if the app is closed;
  • a resized copy of the background photo you choose, if you choose one.

This data is never sent anywhere by the app. Other apps cannot read it.

Photos

If you choose a background photo, you pick it yourself with the Android photo picker. The app receives only that one photo, not access to your gallery, and stores a resized copy for itself. Deleting the original from your gallery does not affect the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It cannot access the internet, your contacts, your location, your microphone or your camera.

Ads, analytics and third parties

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. We receive no information about you or how you use the app.

Device backups

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Google under your own account; we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

  • Training history: History, then Clear history.
  • Colors and background photo: Options, then Reset appearance.
  • Everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your Android settings.

Children

Faceoff Five is intended for users aged 13 and over. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
